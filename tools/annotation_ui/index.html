Annotation guide

Judge the pixels, not the likely label

The amber rectangle identifies the person to label. Do not infer motion from clothing, location, or what the person might do next.
Seated / upright / other
Describe body posture. Use indeterminate when the body is not sufficiently visible.
Stationary
The image contains positive support for a stationary pose; it is not merely missing motion evidence.
Locomoting
The pose or scene provides visible evidence of translation through the environment.
Transition
The person appears to be starting, stopping, standing up, sitting down, or otherwise between states.
Not inferable
A single frame does not contain enough evidence to distinguish stationary from locomoting.
Gait
Select walking or running only with visible locomotion. Stationary + not applicable means the person is not changing location; localized actions such as throwing or hitting may still be occurring. Locomoting + not applicable means the person is changing location by a mode other than walking or running, such as crawling, cycling, skating, or swimming. Use indeterminate when translation is visible and walking versus running cannot be distinguished.
Visibility
Choose the strongest limitation: occluded by another object, cut by the frame boundary, too small to judge, or clear.
